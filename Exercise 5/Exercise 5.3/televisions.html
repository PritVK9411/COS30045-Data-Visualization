Television Energy Consumption

Learn how television characteristics can influence electricity consumption.

Why Does Television Size Matter?

Television screen size is one factor that can be considered when examining energy consumption. Larger screens may have different energy requirements depending on the display technology and television model.

Understanding these differences can help consumers make informed purchasing decisions.

What Does Electricity Cost?

What a TV costs to run depends on its energy use and on the price of electricity. The chart below shows the average wholesale spot price of electricity in Australia from 1998 to 2024. Each dot is one year and the line joins them up, so you can see how the price has moved over time.

The spot price is the wholesale price paid for electricity before retailers add network and other charges, so it is not the same as the rate on a household bill. The average covers the mainland states only, because Snowy and Tasmania do not have data for the full period.

Tips for Choosing a Television

  • Consider the screen size you actually need.
  • Compare energy efficiency information between models.
  • Consider how many hours the television will be used each day.
  • Avoid leaving the television running when nobody is watching.
