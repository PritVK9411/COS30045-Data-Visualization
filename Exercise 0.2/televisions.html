Television Energy Consumption

Televisions are common household appliances in Australia. The amount of electricity a television consumes depends on factors such as its screen size, display technology, brightness and how long it is used each day.

Modern televisions are generally designed to use less electricity than older models. However, televisions that have larger screens or higher brightness settings may require more power.

Factors Affecting Energy Usage

Screen Size

Larger television screens can require more electricity to operate compared with smaller screens.

Usage Time

A television that is used for many hours each day will generally consume more energy.

Display Technology

Different display technologies can have different energy requirements.

Brightness

Higher screen brightness settings may increase the amount of electricity consumed.

Reducing Television Energy Consumption

There are several simple ways to reduce television energy consumption. Users can turn the television off when it is not being watched and use appropriate brightness settings.

Choosing an energy-efficient television and avoiding unnecessary standby power can also help reduce overall household electricity consumption.
