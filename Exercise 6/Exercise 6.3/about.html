Our Purpose

This website was developed to demonstrate how web technologies can be used to communicate information about appliance energy consumption.

The website provides information about household appliances and introduces users to the relationship between appliance characteristics and energy consumption.

Our Focus

One of our main areas of interest is television energy consumption. Television screen size, technology and usage patterns can all be considered when examining electricity consumption.
