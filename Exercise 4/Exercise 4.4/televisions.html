Why Does Television Size Matter?

Television screen size is one factor that can be considered when examining energy consumption. Larger screens may have different energy requirements depending on the display technology and television model.

Understanding these differences can help consumers make informed purchasing decisions.

Tips for Choosing a Television

  • Consider the screen size you actually need.
  • Compare energy efficiency information between models.
  • Consider how many hours the television will be used each day.
  • Avoid leaving the television running when nobody is watching.
