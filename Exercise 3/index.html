Storyboard 1
Issue

Buyers stock 76 TV brands on the floor, but no one knows which ones actual demand is concentrated in.

Demonstrate issue

Show a bar chart of registered models by brand: Samsung, Kogan and LG tower over the rest.

Ideas for overcoming issue

Build a 3-tier stocking guide so buyers weight floor space by real demand, not brand count.

Describe the tiering

Tier 1: Samsung, Kogan, LG (leaders). Tier 2: Hisense, Eko (challengers). Tier 3: 71 niche brands (long tail).

Show the split to prove it

Top 3 brands = 57% of all models. Top 5 = 67%. The other 71 brands share the last third.

Recommendation

Give Samsung, Kogan and LG the most shelf space and promo budget; keep an eye on Hisense as a rising Tier 2 brand.

A large screen uses roughly 7× the energy of a small one each year. Screen size and energy use are strongly correlated (r = 0.86). Explore more on the Televisions page.
Storyboard 2
Issue

Shoppers chase bigger screens as the "upgrade", without realising what it costs to run every year.

Demonstrate issue

Show a scatter plot of screen size vs yearly energy use: a clear upward climb, not a flat line.

Ideas for overcoming issue

Give sales staff a simple size-vs-cost guide to hand to shoppers at the point of sale.

Describe the guide

Group TVs into Small, Medium and Large bands so the cost jump is easy to point to on the floor.

Show the numbers to prove it

Large TVs (70in+) average 462 kWh a year, about 7x the ~64 kWh a small TV uses.

Recommendation

Put star ratings front and centre on large screens, and steer size-conscious buyers to the most efficient large models.
