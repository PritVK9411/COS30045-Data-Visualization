Why Does Television Size Matter?

Television screen size is one factor that can be considered when examining energy consumption. Larger screens may have different energy requirements depending on the display technology and television model.

Understanding these differences can help consumers make informed purchasing decisions.

A large screen (70"+) uses roughly 4× the energy of a small one every year. Screen size and energy use are strongly correlated (r = 0.86) across the 4,508 registered models. Curious what that means in dollars? Try the calculator on the Home page.

About this data: figures above come from the Australian Government's Energy Rating product registration database (GEMS Register), filtered to models marked "Available" and sold in Australia (4,508 of 4,724 total registered models). Brand names were cleaned and merged (e.g. "KOGAN", "Kogan" and "kogan" combined) before counting. It records what manufacturers register for sale, not actual household purchases or real-world viewing hours, so treat it as a picture of the market rather than a survey of homes. Tips for Choosing a Television

  • Consider the screen size you actually need.
  • Compare energy efficiency information between models.
  • Consider how many hours the television will be used each day.
  • Avoid leaving the television running when nobody is watching.
